# Content Creation and Posting Plan

## Goals (confirm or change)
1. Generate quote requests/calls from Middle Tennessee.
2. Build proof (reviews, before/afters).
3. Be the recognizable local name for carpet and floor care.

## Audience lanes
- **Residential:** homeowners, renters, pet owners, parents (carpet, upholstery, mattress, auto interior).
- **Commercial:** offices, property managers, restaurants, churches, schools (carpet, floors).

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Before & After transformations | 35% | Stain removal, high-traffic lane, mattress, car seat; time-lapse and split-screen |
| Education and tips | 25% | How often to clean carpet, pet-stain myths, what "fast dry" means, DIY mistakes |
| Process and trust | 15% | The five-step process, equipment, licensed and insured, crew intros |
| Social proof | 15% | Review screenshots, customer clips, job stories |
| Offers and local | 10% | Seasonal specials, service-area shout-outs, free estimate CTA |

## Cadence (realistic for a solo manager)
| Platform | Per week | Formats | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 3 Reels + 2 Stories days + 1 carousel | Reels, carousels, Stories | Transformations, tips |
| TikTok | 3-4 | Short video (reuse Reels) | Satisfying cleaning, reach |
| Facebook | 3-4 | Reposted Reels, photo albums, local groups | Local discovery, reviews, homeowners |
| Google Business Profile | 1-2 | Photo and update posts | Local search and calls |

**Time budget:** ~2 hours filming on job days (capture, don't stage), ~1.5 hours editing and scheduling per week. Batch-film once a week.

## Caption template
1. **Hook** (first line, a result or question): "This carpet hadn't been cleaned in 8 years."
2. **Story** (1-2 lines): what it was, what we did.
3. **Proof/benefit:** dry in X hours, safe for pets, etc.
4. **CTA:** "Free estimate: link in bio or call (615) 510-8451."
5. **Hashtags (5-8):** #nashvillecleaning #murfreesboro #smyrnatn #carpetcleaning #beforeandafter #middletennessee #upholsterycleaning #cleaningtiktok

## Production checklist per job
- Photo or video **before**, **during**, **after** from the same angle and lighting.
- 10-second vertical clip of the cleaning action (wand pass, extraction).
- Ask the customer for permission and a quick review on the spot.
- Never show house numbers, faces of kids, or personal items.

## Engagement
- Reply to every comment and DM within 24 hours (business days).
- Join local Facebook community groups where allowed; answer cleaning questions helpfully.
- Comment on local business posts a few times a week.
