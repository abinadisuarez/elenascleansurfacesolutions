## Strengths
- Clear, specific local service area, which suits local-SEO and geo-tagged content.
- Handles are consistent across Instagram and TikTok (@elenasclean).
- Strong, concrete differentiators (five-step process, fast dry times, licensed and insured) that translate well into content.
- Services are very visual (stains, dirty-to-clean), a natural fit for short video.

## Gaps and risks
1. **Name inconsistency.** "Elena's Clean" vs "Elena's Clean Surface Solutions". Pick one display name for bios and captions and use the other as a descriptor.
2. **Weak visual identity.** Black and white with a text logo gives no recognizable accent color, so feeds will look generic next to competitors. Add one or two accent colors (fresh blue/teal suits cleaning) and a consistent photo style.
3. **No social proof on the site.** No testimonials, reviews, or before/after gallery. This is the biggest conversion gap and the best content source.
4. **Mixed audience message.** "Commercial carpet cleaning" leads, but mattress, upholstery and auto detailing are residential/consumer services. Decide whether social targets homeowners, businesses, or both (recommended: two lanes, see plan).
5. **No pricing signal.** Consider "starting at" or example-job pricing in content to filter and pre-qualify leads.
6. **"Specialty cleaning" is vague.** Name specifics (pet stains, wine, grease, move-out, post-construction) so it can be searched and posted about.
7. **Language.** Verify whether Spanish-language content would reach more of the local market; the site is English only as far as reviewed.
8. **Verify on each social profile:** bio uses the phone, city, hours and booking link; link-in-bio goes to a quote page; profile photo is the logo; Facebook business category, service area and hours are filled in; a Google Business Profile exists and matches name, address and phone exactly.

## Quick wins (this week)
- Standardize bio: `Carpet, floor & upholstery cleaning | Middle Tennessee | Free estimates` plus phone and quote link.
- Create 4-5 highlight/pinned categories: Before & After, Services, Process, Reviews, FAQ.
- Pin a post that explains the five-step process.
- Ask the last 10 customers for a Google review and a short video or photo; reuse with permission.
- Add UTM-tagged quote links per platform to measure leads.
